Arms: `warm` (run and discarded, A31), then `A`, `B`, `A2` -- a repeat brackets
the treatment. The warm-* files are deliberately not analysed.

Result: pre-registered rule was "f64 must beat the floor's geomean with no dtype
regressing beyond it". It does not. B/A reads 0.941 in f64 against a floor
(A'/A) of 0.990-0.999, and three of the four dtype columns *cannot* be touched
by the switch yet moved 3.7-5.9%, which is what makes the run readable: the B
arm ran under roughly double the L3-domain co-tenant load of A and A'. Corrected
by its in-arm control, f64 is about 0.977 -- neutral to negative, and nowhere
near the +2.9% the grid had suggested.

Why it matters: this closes Phase 4 item 2. The grid's +2.9% and Zen2's +3.1%
agreed because both grids' `base` arm was the contaminated one, so every
`arm / base` ratio was inflated identically. The "agreement across two machines"
was a shared artefact, not a replication. `TENSORCONTRACT_DEEPEN` stays
off by default as a record of the experiment.

Honest limit: the machine was not quiet. Every arm shows 6-14 co-tenants in its
L3 domain -- read the .cpu files, not just the means. The controls permit the
correction and the corrected verdict would have to be wrong by 5 points to
reverse the decision, so a repeat on an idle machine is confirmatory rather than
necessary.

THE DESIGN, AND WHY IT IS BETTER THAN MOST OF THIS REPOSITORY'S A/Bs

  Four arms from ONE binary, the treatment being TENSORCONTRACT_KERNEL:

    warm.csv        discarded. A laptop's first arm pays for cold caches and a
                    clock ramping onto AC.
    A-scalar.csv    control
    B-neon.csv      treatment
    A2-scalar.csv   control again. A vs A2 IS THIS SESSION'S FLOOR.

  This satisfies A15 (never diff two builds) and A20 without a rebuild, which
  the equivalent x86 question cannot do: an AVX-512 machine cannot un-have its
  own kernels. Both code paths are compiled into the same executable and the
  switch chooses at run time.

  ttgt and tblis ride along in every arm. NO setting of TENSORCONTRACT_KERNEL
  can reach either, so any movement there is drift and bounds the engine
  columns from outside. They moved 0.996-1.006. That is the second control.

THE FLOOR (this session, 1 thread, A against A2)

  engine geomeans   1.000 - 1.008      <- quote ratios against this
  baseline geomeans 0.982 - 1.004
  per case          9 of 120 outside +/-6%; worst ENGINE case 1.105
                    (abjcd-dkbac-jk, the slowest shape in the set)

  So a geomean ratio is readable to about +/-1%, and a per-case ratio to about
  +/-10%. The treatment below is 1.69-1.84, i.e. 80-100x the geomean floor, and
  its per-case minimum (1.362) clears the per-case floor comfortably.

  STILL NOT A PINNED MEASUREMENT. Darwin has no CPU affinity API and none can
  be written; every other floor in this repository is pinned and this one is
  not. See DECISIONS.md part 20.

THE TREATMENT -- 12 premise shapes, 64 MiB, reps 3, 1 thread, geomean GF/s

                      scalar    NEON    ratio
    f64  (all three)   19.90   36.60    1.840   <- one real kernel, three names
    c64  planar        24.34   44.46    1.826
    c64  1m            23.31   41.74    1.791
    c64  3m            29.85   50.45    1.690
    -- controls the switch cannot reach --
    f64  OpenBLAS TTGT 31.62   31.51    0.996
    f64  TBLIS 2.0-dev 36.73   36.76    1.001
    c64  OpenBLAS TTGT 35.20   35.42    1.006
    c64  TBLIS 2.0-dev 44.97   45.19    1.005

  A58 predicted ~2x from FMA contraction alone and this is 1.84. Per case the
  range is 1.586-2.028 in f64. The earlier one-case --size 8 probe read 1.91;
  at the published size and over 12 shapes it is 1.84, so that probe was right
  in direction and slightly high in magnitude -- which is the good outcome A46
  asks you to check for rather than assume.

AGAINST THE BASELINES, AND THIS IS THE HEADLINE

                        scalar          NEON
    f64 engine / TTGT    0.63           1.16
    f64 engine / TBLIS   0.54           1.00     (36.60 vs 36.76)
    c64 planar / TBLIS   0.54           0.98
    c64 3m     / TBLIS   0.66           1.12
    c64 3m     / TTGT    0.85           1.42

  Best-shape efficiency against the 64.8 GF/s P-core NEON FMA peak
  (4.05 GHz, 2 lanes, 2 FMA pipes, 2 flops/FMA):

    engine   52.76 of 64.8 = 81.4%   (ijkl-imjn-nlmk, f64)
    OpenBLAS 56.88 of 64.8 = 87.8%
    TBLIS    58.18 of 64.8 = 89.8%

  Part 20 measured the scalar path at 80.5% of a ceiling HALVED to 32.4 by the
  missing fmla. The NEON kernel reaches 81.4% of the FULL ceiling. Same
  efficiency, twice the ceiling: A58's mechanism, confirmed by removing it.

  THE 0.46x FIGURE IS SUPERSEDED. Part 20 carried a provisional "0.46x of TBLIS"
  from a one-case --size 8 probe and labelled it as such. At 64 MiB over 12
  shapes the scalar path is 0.54 of TBLIS 2.0-dev, and the NEON path is 1.00.
  Direction right, magnitude 17% off -- quote 0.54, and only for the scalar arm.

THE COMPLEX-METHOD RANKING: 3m STILL LEADS, AND A59 GUESSED WRONG

    3m / planar   scalar 1.226  ->  NEON 1.135

  A59 expected the ordering to move or invert once a real FMA kernel removed
  the instruction-throughput bottleneck that explained 3m's lead. It NARROWED
  and did not invert: 3m gains least from NEON (1.690 against planar's 1.826),
  exactly as the mechanism predicts, and still finishes ahead.

  So this is a FOURTH ordering, and the most interesting one, because it is the
  only one taken with a properly tuned kernel on the machine: Cascade Lake has
  3m leading only at L1-resident kc, Ice Lake has 3m last in every column and
  winning 0 of 49, the M3 Max scalar path had 3m leading by 1.226, and the M3
  Max NEON path has 3m leading by 1.135. A44 is not reopened -- the ranking
  remains per-microarchitecture -- but "3m is a fair-comparison method, not a
  candidate default" is now a statement about x86 specifically.

  A one-case --size 8 probe had suggested 3m's lead WIDENS under NEON. At 64
  MiB it narrows. A46, a third time.

  In f64 all three methods read 36.60 exactly, because they select the same
  real kernel. A control on the harness, not a coincidence.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW

  * Nothing per-case at finer than ~10%, and nothing about --stress ragged,
    which is the only source of irregularity on this machine.
  * Nothing about any other aarch64 part. Register blocks are
    per-microarchitecture (A34) and these were measured on this one.
  * Nothing about threads. Single-threaded throughout, and D46 keeps threads
    off by default anyway.
  * No 49-case corpus sweep. These are the 12 premise shapes. The corpus arms
    are the still-unfinished half of the Stage A session; see the parent
    directory's PROVENANCE.txt.

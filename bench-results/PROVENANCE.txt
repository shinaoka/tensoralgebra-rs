PROVENANCE -- bench-results/ loose files (Phase 2b and Phase 3)
================================================================

Machine   : ccqlin038 (Flatiron CCQ). Xeon Gold 6244, Cascade Lake, AVX-512,
            2 x 8 cores, 1 MiB L2/core, 25 MiB L3/socket.
Date      : 2026-08-02, except methods-f64c64.csv (2026-08-01, Phase 2b).
Job       : none -- workstation runs, before this project used Slurm.
Produced  : scripts/phase3-bench.sh, except methods-f64c64.csv.
Engine    : the Phase 3 engine. Predates EVERY Phase 4 gain.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
Everything else in bench-results/ is a directory with its own PROVENANCE.txt.
These nine files sit loose at the top level for historical reasons -- they were
written before the <host>-<uarch>/<experiment>/ convention existed -- and the
project's own rule is "read the PROVENANCE.txt next to a CSV before quoting it".
There was no file to read. This is it. The per-file descriptions are in
README.md's "Loose files at the top level" table and are not repeated here.

WHAT THESE ARE GOOD FOR
-----------------------
* phase3-premise-*.csv is the evidence for the project's headline finding: the
  complex-over-real efficiency ratio is 0.215 against TBLIS v1.3.0 and 1.06
  against 2.0-dev. That result is `settled` and was replicated on Ice Lake.
* phase3-kernel-shapes.txt is the AVX-512 register-block sweep. The shipped
  blocks come from it, and it is where A35 was found -- months after the fact,
  at no machine cost.
* phase3-sweep-*.csv is a full 49-case corpus run against TBLIS 2.0-dev and
  TTGT on Cascade Lake.

WHAT THEY CANNOT SUPPORT
------------------------
* NOT the current engine's performance. They predate the write-back orientation
  fix, the row-block rule, the orientation rule and everything else in Phase 4.
* NOT comparable with worker6156-icelake/, which holds the newest
  engine-vs-baseline numbers. Different microarchitecture; no number measured on
  one machine may be differenced against one from another. That is precisely why
  "what Phase 4 bought end to end" is still an open question -- see
  docs/open-questions.md.
* NOT an exclusive-machine measurement. ccqlin038 is a shared workstation and
  these runs did not reserve it. Treat per-case values as indicative.
* NOT preceded by a discarded warm-up arm: they predate A31, which is the
  assumption that established warm-up as necessary. phase3-bench.sh is marked
  superseded in scripts/README.md for this reason, among others.

Read with: docs/results.md (the findings), docs/measurement-rules.md (the floors
and why they are per-session), and README.md in this directory.

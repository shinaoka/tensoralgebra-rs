What is in it: `bl-<arm>-<dtype>` for 19 blocking arms in two dtype pairs, a
discarded warm-up grid under `warmup/`, `features.csv`, `score.txt` from
`scripts/blocking-score-rules.py` and `placement-audit.txt` from
`scripts/grid-placement-audit.py`.

Why it matters: this is the reference machine's answer to the same grid
`worker5040-zen2/phase4e/` ran on Zen2, and it *reverses* it. Pinned `kc = 512`
is worth 1.050 on Zen2 and 0.961 here, so it is a machine-specific result and
must not ship. The analytical model costs 14% (f64) to 34% (c32 1m) here against
2-7% on Zen2 -- its `kc` sinks it on Zen2 and its `mc` sinks it here, so both
halves of the derivation are wrong, on different machines.

The `base` arm in this grid was itself 1-2% slow (`basem`/`base2` read
1.010-1.020), which inflates every `arm / base` ratio in it. That contamination
is the reason the +2.9% here and the +3.1% on Zen2 agreed, and it was only
caught by the end-to-end A/B in `../ab-deepen/`. Read the two together;
this directory alone will mislead you in the optimistic direction.

Note: the naming here is host-prefixed while the same experiment on Zen2 sits at
`worker5040-zen2/phase4e/`. See ../README.md for the two schemes.

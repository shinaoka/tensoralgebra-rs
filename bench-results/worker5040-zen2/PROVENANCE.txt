Why it matters, in one line each:

  * All eight AVX2 register blocks were confirmed as the measured winners, so
    D26's guesses stopped being guesses with no code change. `kernel-shapes.txt`
    is that measurement. The margins over the runner-up are 0.8-1.6%; the
    margins over the *rejected* shapes are 20-35%, so the choice was never
    between close alternatives.
  * Thread scaling: 8 scales, 16-32 saturates, 64 declines with cores idle
    two-thirds of the time. The 2-D partition is vindicated at 1.6-3.4x, but
    `Plan::partition`'s `panels >= p` early return costs up to 4.3x on 18 of 49
    cases here -- and that cost is *absent* on Ice Lake (A36), so the branch is
    right where threads share one L3 and wrong where they span many.
  * The concurrent one-arm-per-L3-domain placement was validated here (+0.3% on
    the corpus) and rejected on the memory-bound half (-3.2%, up to -10%), which
    is A27 and is why the traffic-sensitive arms were re-run sequentially in
    ../worker5175-zen2/.

Not comparable to anything measured on ccqlin038: different machine, different
cache hierarchy, and AVX2 rather than AVX-512. Every ratio here is
within-session against a floor re-derived here.

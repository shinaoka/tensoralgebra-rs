Why it matters: this is the confirmation, not a replication for its own sake.
`PARTITION_SWEEP` prices the partition axis against thread count on the
`abcijk` family, and the effect tracks **how many L3 domains the thread set
spans**, not the thread count: 4 threads in 1 domain reads 1.031, 16 threads in
4 domains 1.173, 64 threads in 16 domains 2.004. Replicated in all four dtypes,
with f32 reaching 2.38x. That is what makes A36 a mechanism rather than a
correlation between two machines.

It also reproduced two things independently: worker5040's scaling curve (t64:
f64 5.54 against 5.37, c64 7.69 against 7.65) and A31's cold-start artefact, its
own t1/t1b floor coming back at 0.954-0.973 on a third node.

Not comparable to anything measured on ccqlin038.

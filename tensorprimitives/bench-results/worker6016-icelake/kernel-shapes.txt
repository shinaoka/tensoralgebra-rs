Micro-kernel register-block sweep, packed panels hot.
Useful flops: 2*MR*NR*kc real, 8*MR*NR*kc complex (so 3m's saving shows up as GF/s).
kc is swept because it decides where the A sliver lives: at kc=16 both panels are
L1-resident and the kernel is purely issue-limited; at kc=256 the A sliver is an L2
stream, which is what the driver actually does. The gap between the two columns is
the kernel's exposure to L2 bandwidth, and it is what the `B/flop` column predicts.
